FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are prices inclusive of VAT?

No, all listed prices exclude VAT (15%). Tax will be added at invoice issuance.

Can I try the system for free?

Yes, we offer a free trial period. Contact our sales team to get started.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes, you can upgrade at any time and the difference will be calculated on a pro-rata basis.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept bank transfers, credit cards, and Mada. Contact sales for details.

Does the subscription include technical support?

Yes, all plans include 24/7 technical support, 7 days a week.
