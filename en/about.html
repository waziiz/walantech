Our Story

Since Walan Technology was founded in 1994, our goal has been clear: to build technological solutions that help businesses grow, evolve, and make the most of their potential.

Throughout our long journey, we have contributed to developing integrated business systems and solutions serving multiple sectors — from small and medium enterprises to large corporations — with a focus on delivering a reliable, easy-to-use technology experience capable of keeping pace with the rapid changes in the business world.

We don't just provide software — we provide long-term technology solutions and partnerships that help our clients improve performance, enhance operational efficiency, make more accurate decisions, and achieve sustainable expansion with confidence.

Today we continue our journey toward the future, drawing inspiration from Saudi Vision 2030, and believing that technology is the primary driver of business growth and success in the digital age.

🔭 Our Vision

To be the first choice for businesses seeking a trusted technology partner that supports their growth, expansion, and leads their digital transformation toward a more successful and sustainable future.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver advanced technological solutions and professional services that empower our clients to achieve efficiency, flexibility, and innovation in all aspects of their business.
